Good notes are not a transcript. They are a map back to an idea.

## During the lecture

I capture definitions, examples, and questions—but I leave space rather than racing to record every sentence. A star marks anything the lecturer emphasizes; a question mark marks the places where my understanding is incomplete.

## Within twenty-four hours

I rewrite the difficult sections in plain language. If I cannot explain a concept without copying the slide, I probably do not understand it yet. This is also when I add one worked example and connect the lecture to earlier material.

## Before an exam

The notes become a set of prompts, not a document to reread passively. I hide the explanation, attempt the question, and check the gaps. The website version comes last: publishing forces me to remove ambiguity and makes the work reusable.
