A personal website does not have to begin with a grand announcement. It can begin with a useful note.

I wanted a place that could hold more than a conventional portfolio: lecture notes next to side projects, technical explanations next to hobbies, and polished work next to ideas that are still taking shape. A **digital garden** is a helpful way to think about it. Posts can be improved as my understanding changes.

## What belongs here?

The short answer is anything I would be glad to find again:

- coursework rewritten in my own words;
- project decisions, including the ones that did not work;
- explanations of small concepts that finally clicked;
- recommendations and notes from life away from a screen.

The goal is not to publish constantly. It is to make each published thing worth keeping.

## A small promise

I will prefer clarity over sounding clever, curiosity over certainty, and progress over polish. This website will change as I do—and that is the point.
