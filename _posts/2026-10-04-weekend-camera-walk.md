The useful constraint of a camera walk is that the destination does not matter. Attention does.

I try to take one lens and spend an hour within walking distance of home. Familiar streets become exercises in geometry, light, repetition, and timing. Most photographs are unremarkable. A few reveal something I had passed many times without really seeing.

That habit carries back into technical work: slow down, change your angle, and look at the edges of the frame.
