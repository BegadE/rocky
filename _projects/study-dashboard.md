## The idea

Most task managers are built to hold every kind of work. I wanted something narrower: a dashboard that answers three questions—what is due, what needs attention, and whether I am allocating time realistically.

## What I’m exploring

- A deliberately small data model
- Local-first storage
- A weekly review instead of constant notifications
- Useful progress signals without turning studying into a game
